Fuel, afterburner & returning to base

Choose your fuel load before launch. A lighter aircraft handles more sharply, but has less endurance. Watch the FUEL and ENGINE readouts: afterburner spends fuel quickly. The airbrake stays deployed until you press X again, so check its indicator before takeoff.

Fly within 250 m of your own/home airport runway, taxiway or apron to restock weapons and countermeasures. Distance includes altitude. An enemy airport is not a service station. A low pass does not repair damage: land at home with gear down, stop below 15 knots, reduce throttle to 20% or less and wait for repairs and refueling. Flybys do not refill fuel. Weapons and countermeasures restock only at your home base, not automatically during combat. Leave the service area before making another resupply pass.

Combat & energy management

Guns fire straight ahead. Within 1 km, the dotted lead ring predicts where to aim; bring the crosshair onto it. Keep your target near the crosshair to build missile lock. Q fires a heat seeker after a short lock; E fires a radar missile after a full lock. Missile keys fire once per press, not while held.

Amber means an enemy is acquiring you; red means a missile is airborne. Against heat seekers, cut power, turn and use F for flares. Against radar missiles, use C for chaff and evade. Countermeasures can fail, especially against heat missiles when using afterburner. In the bomber, use B when the predicted impact circle turns mint.

Climbs and hard turns cost speed. Diving builds it. At low speed, ease off the turn and add power to avoid a stall. Sustained severe G can narrow vision or briefly black out the view; ease the controls to recover. Pause stays available. Aircraft can collide, including wingmates.

Missions, cameras & keyboard navigation

All pilots depart from airports; wait for runway clearance if your squad is ahead. In bot skirmishes, R rejoins the same match after a short recovery. Escort, Intercept and Ambush missions have no respawns. Follow the marked transport and mission objective. In free flight, L toggles route assist. Flight keys temporarily override it.

V switches between chase and cockpit views. The cockpit attitude display shows aircraft orientation against the horizon. Tab and Shift+Tab move between menu controls; Enter or Space activates buttons, and arrow keys change native selections. Escape closes dialogs or pauses flight.
