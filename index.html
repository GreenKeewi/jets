The gun fires straight ahead. Within 1 km, a small dotted ring predicts where to aim at your selected target; bring the crosshair onto it. Brief center ticks confirm gun hits. Tap power keys to fine-tune, or hold for a fast throttle sweep; airspeed still builds gradually. Fly within 250 m of an airfield runway, taxiway or apron to refill all weapons, flares and chaff. Resupply does not repair airframe damage. Distance includes altitude, so make a low pass. Leave beyond 350 m before another pass; full resupply has a 12-second cooldown. Small gun and missile top-ups still occur during the sortie. Missile keys fire once per press; holding Q/E does not steer or repeat launches. Heat seeker inbound → cut power, deploy flares, and evade. Flares can fail; afterburner makes a heat seeker much harder to fool. Radar missile inbound → chaff. Keep targets near your crosshair to build a lock. Amber means an enemy is acquiring you. Red means a missile is airborne: turn hard and use F for heat or C for radar. Missiles can overshoot; they are not guaranteed hits. Aircraft can collide, including wingmates. In bot skirmishes, R returns you to the same match after a short recovery. Air combat also offers Escort, Intercept and Survive an ambush: follow the objective and marked transport, then retry from the result or pause menu. These timed missions have no respawns. Transport seats remain AI-controlled in multiplayer. Cautious pilots extend away sooner, aggressive pilots press the attack, and paired pilots share a pursuit target. Smoke marks damaged aircraft; the brief airframe message names incoming damage and its bearing. Lock advisories are slow; heat warnings fall in pitch and radar warnings rise in three notes. Nearby flybys move through stereo sound. Choose Northern valley under Battle area for low-altitude squad fights and valley respawns. Climbing trades speed for height; diving builds speed. Hard turns add drag, so ease off and add power before you run out of energy. Low airspeed reduces climb and can cause a stall sink. Power around 78% cruises near 550 knots in level flight; full power can reach Mach 2. At high speed, turns and pull-ups are wider: slow down before a tight fight. Brief hard turns leave vision clear. Prolonged severe G causes gentle tunnel vision and eventually a brief blackout; ease the turn for quick recovery and automatic control return. Pause always stays available. In cockpit view, the moving dashes mark the world horizon; UP marks its sky side and the central plus stays fixed. In strike mode, the ground circle is your predicted bomb impact; release when it turns mint.
