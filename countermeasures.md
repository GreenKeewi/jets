# Countermeasure loadouts and arcade behavior

Research checked public USAF and manufacturer material in October 2026. There is no single universal F-16 flare/chaff count across variants and installations. [BAE's ALE-47 brochure](https://www.baesystems.com/dam/jcr%3Aecb3a7c5-8568-4a0d-9da5-6339ef33c11f/16-D99-02-ALE-47-brochure-2025-w.pdf) lists F-16 compatibility and configurable platform integration, but does not establish an aircraft-specific expendable total. [Terma's F-16 pylon brochure](https://www.terma.com/media/5kapzsva/pylon-based-ew-solutions-letter-feb2022.pdf) documents PIDS variants with three magazines per equipped pylon and a Flare-Up Modification that increases flare capacity. This is a magazine count, not a flare count. [Terma's ACMDS brochure](https://www.terma.com/media/rgtd5ius/advanced-countermeasures-dispenser-system-letter-v1-low.pdf) documents mixed payloads, multi-shot cartridges, and different F-16 integrations. A cartridge, magazine and individual expelled decoy are therefore not interchangeable units; real flare/chaff mixes depend on configuration.

The [USAF B-2 public fact sheet](https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/104482/b-2-spirit/) describes low observability but does not publish a flare/chaff capacity. No B-2 capacity was verified in the authoritative public sources reviewed. We do not infer a classified capacity or claim the game's dispensers represent installed real B-2 hardware.

The game therefore explicitly uses **fictional balanced inventories**: F-16-inspired fighter **60 flare releases and 60 chaff releases**; B-2-inspired bomber **120 and 120**. One displayed unit is one game release. Six visual particles illustrate each release and do not multiply inventory or seeker attempts. This is a generous arcade loadout, not a claim of verified aircraft counts or one-to-one War Thunder/simulator fidelity.

Default F releases flares against heat seekers; C releases chaff against radar seekers; camera remains V. All remain rebindable. Each discrete key press spends one available release immediately with no gameplay cooldown. Held keys and OS repeat do not repeatedly dispense. Counted queues retain rapid presses that land between animation/network ticks; transport replay/rate/queue safety limits remain to prevent malformed traffic and unbounded memory. HOME restock restores each aircraft's finite stocks.

Each fresh matching burst gives a new chance. A failed burst is remembered for that missile so its visual particles and subsequent frames cannot reroll. Later attempts use 1/(1 + 0.65 × prior attempts) of the initial chance. This gives diminishing per-burst returns while increasing cumulative chances. Heat chances retain existing power/afterburner balance; radar chaff starts at 64%. Wrong-type decoys do nothing. Repeated releases can still all fail; this is fictional game probability, not real tactics. The requested cinematic bomber SAM exception remains guaranteed for a fresh bomber flare.

Original warm flare sparks and broader cool chaff clouds have finite lifetimes. At most 240 countermeasure particles remain active/rendered, and peer snapshots further trim cosmetic arrays if needed to fit the existing 60 kB wire budget. Host simulation retains authoritative inventory and missile outcomes. AI counter decisions already consume a pending flare once or select a single chaff release per 3–7 second threat-response decision; player cooldown removal does not make AI hold the dispenser continuously.
